# Rubric: [Link](https://github.com/jackrosenthal/algobowl/blob/main/docs/rules.md)

- Valid Input Submission: 5 points
- Input: 10 points
  - The input grade will be determined based on the maximum of the input difficulty score and the input creation description
- Ranking: 15 points
  - There are always many questions about the "Ranking" portion of the grade. The short explanation is that there will be two benchmarks provided by the TAs. If a team passes only the first benchmark, they earn 5 points. If a team passes the second benchmark, they earn 10 points. This means that all teams are eligible to score at least 10 out of 15 of the ranking points. The final 5 points are the only points that are impacted by actual ranking in competition against other teams. Consequently, this also implies (assuming everything else was done correctly) that all student teams could theoretically earn an A with 95% or higher on this project if they put in the effort to match the second TA benchmark.
- Verification: 20 points
- Valid Submission: 50 Points

# Input Upload: Monday, Apr 6, 10pm

## Valid Input Submission: 5 Points

All teams design one input and upload it to the AlgoBOWL website. The input format will automatically be verified by the website software.
Teams without a valid input uploaded at this time will receive 0 out of 15 possible points for input upload. However, they will receive an automatically-generated random input enabling them to continue participating in the rest of the competition.

# Output Upload: Thursday, Apr 9, 10pm

Inputs from each team become available for every team to download. Each team downloads the inputs from all other teams and produces an output for each input. The output must be uploaded to the AlgoBOWL website by the due date.

Note: No automatic verification is preformed. It is very important that you submit outputs which meet the specification; see the next stage for how verification is preformed.

-
-

## Output Verification (Optional): Friday, Apr 10, 10pm

Each team downloads the outputs that all the other teams produced for their input. They use their verification software to mark the output as valid or invalid.

-
-

## Resolution (Optional): Saturday, Apr 11, 10pm

If the verifying team rejects your output, you will be sent an email with two options, which must be completed within 24 hours:

You can upload a new output. The instructional team and TAs will verify the new output. This will come at the cost of one penalty point. If you submit
a new output which is rejected, the rejection will be permanent and you will get no chance to reupload.

You can protest the verification. The instructional team will verify your original output manually. If your output was correct, your output will be
marked as accepted without penalty, and the verifying team will receive a re-duction on the verification portion of their grade. If your output was incorrect, the rejection will be permanent and you will have no chance to reupload.

-
-

## Open Verification (Optional): Tuesday, Apr 14, 10pm

At this time, all outputs for all inputs will become available. Teams which question the accuracy of the verification of other teams may wish to download outputs. If they discover inaccuracies, they may submit a protest and the team which did not verify correctly will have the verification portion of their grade reduced.

-
-

## Group Eval (Optional): Tuesday, Apr 14, 10pm

Each individual submits the effort contribution for each of their group members. This is used in the determination of the grade for the project; if it is not completed, we assume all teammates contributed equally.
